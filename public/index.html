Getting started with Vega

Vega runs on your computer. Each installation should have its own project list, session history, and credentials. Folders are not discovered automatically.

  1. Connect a model through OmniRoute. Configure a provider in your local OmniRoute setup. Free cloud models can be rate-limited or unavailable, so no free model can be promised to work all the time.
  2. Choose your folders. Register only folders you own and want Vega to access. The Copilot agent can read ordinary files in that folder; proposed edits wait for your approval.
  3. Know what leaves your computer. If OmniRoute uses a cloud provider, your prompts and any files the agent reads may be sent to that provider. Check its privacy terms before sharing sensitive material.
  4. Start with optional features off. Voice, background listening, automations, and project work are separate. Enable them only after reviewing their settings.

A clean download must not include another person's project registry, local state, or keys. Keep your own data in your own installation.
